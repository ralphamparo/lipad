How it works

  • We check through the day. When a fare matching your alert drops below your price, you get one email with the dates and a link straight to it.
  • No spam. You confirm the alert by email first, and we send at most one message every few days per alert — and only when the price beats the last one we told you about.
  • One click to stop. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works immediately.
  • Be realistic with the price. Set it near the cheapest fare you've seen on that route, not far below it, or the alert may never fire. The deals page shows what each route currently costs.
