Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

Lipad shows flight prices. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, or payment details. Bookings happen on the airline or booking site, not here.

What we collect

  • Your search settings — departure airport, dates, number of adults and so on. These are kept in the web address of the page so you can share or bookmark a search. They are not tied to you.
  • Basic server logs — our server records requests (including IP address) to keep the site running and to spot abuse.
  • Nothing else. We do not sell data, and we do not ask for or store personal details.

Services we use

  • Travelpayouts / Aviasales supplies the fares. When you tap through to a fare, you leave Lipad for their site, where their own privacy policy applies. If you book, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Google AdSense may show ads on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. You can manage this at My Ad Center and read Google's practices at How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

Cookies and storage

Lipad itself does not set advertising cookies. Your browser may store the app's files so it works offline after you install it. Advertising cookies, if any, come from Google as described above.

Children

Lipad is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
